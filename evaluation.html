05 · Dataset Preprocessing & Training

From heterogeneous MRI data to a reliable BrainVLM

Multi-source processing, unified curation, and progressive 2D–3D training.

BrainVLM combines public repositories, web-based medical resources, and multi-institutional clinical data. A structured processing pipeline harmonizes these sources before staged training teaches the model MRI–text representations, volumetric reasoning, and calibrated diagnostic behavior.

47,947 individuals2D & 3D MRIThree-stage trainingReliability-aware inference

Multi-source dataset preprocessing

Different sources require different quality-control paths before they can contribute consistent MRI–text supervision.

Process

Three routes, one standardized representation

Structured repositories are registered and paired with tumor location and clinical metadata. Web resources undergo slice separation, irrelevant-content filtering, and slice–caption matching. Private radiology reports are cleaned, translated, and reformatted with clinician-validated templates and medical lexicons.

Structured sourcesRegistration & metadata extraction
Web resourcesFiltering & caption matching
Clinical reportsCleaning & expert validation

BrainTumor48K dataset curation

The processed sources are assembled into complementary 2D and 3D resources rather than being reduced to a single homogeneous task.

Curate

Image–text scale with volumetric clinical depth

BrainTumor48K integrates large 2D image–caption collections with 3D MRI volumes linked to diagnoses, radiology reports, tumor masks, signal characteristics, locations, and patient metadata when available. This mixture supports both broad representation learning and clinically grounded volumetric reasoning.

Cohort scale47,947 individuals
Visual formats2D slices & 3D volumes
SupervisionCaptions, diagnosis, reports & metadata

Progressive training and diagnostic reliability

Training gradually increases the dimensionality and clinical specificity of the task, then adds explicit supervision for confidence-aware diagnosis.

Train

From slice–text alignment to reliable volumetric reasoning

Stage 1 learns 2D MRI slice–text representations. Stage 2 combines 2D and 3D examples to bridge slice-level and case-level understanding. Stage 3 performs volumetric instruction tuning on higher-quality 3D data. Consensus-driven response clustering then supports reliability training and confidence-triggered Top-2 diagnostic candidates for ambiguous cases.

Why this matters. The data pipeline preserves the clinical context needed for report generation, while progressive training lets BrainVLM move from large-scale visual–language alignment toward patient-level volumetric diagnosis and interpretable uncertainty.
